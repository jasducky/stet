What the quarter actually showed

Northwick Analytics · a worked example for artefact-review

This document exists so you can try the tool on something. Open it with python3 server.py examples/quarterly-note.html, hover any paragraph, and edit it. Your change is written straight back into this file as a byte-range replacement, so the diff stays readable and nothing outside the edited span moves.

Everything in it is invented. Northwick Analytics is a fictional company and no figure below describes a real operation.

The finding

Eleven depots, fourteen weeks. The distribution is the finding, not the average. Most handovers take forty minutes and a handful take six hours, and the average sits in the middle describing neither.

The long ones are almost always a paperwork mismatch rather than anything physical, which matters because a paperwork problem is fixable by a decision and a queue is not.

The yard does not have a waiting problem. It has a decision-making problem that expresses itself as waiting.

What we would do differently

The first build ranked depots against one another. It was accurate and it was ignored for six weeks, because a ranking tells a supervisor their standing and not their next action.

The second build listed the handovers still open and nothing else. It was used the first morning it existed.
